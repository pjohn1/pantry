## Design Specificity Verdict

**The paint is authored. The mechanics are not.**

Warm Paper could not be worn unchanged by another product, and the measurement backs the discipline up: zero `text-transform: uppercase` in `world-kanban.css`, zero hardcoded colours outside the two documented exceptions, zero focusable inputs under 16px, reduced-motion coverage 14/14 with no gap, every `--kb-*` text pair passing AA in both themes.

Underneath it is a generic CRUD list. Three proofs:

- **Derivation is a button below the fold.** `renderFooter()` appends "Refresh list" after every category group. Add ten staples in Settings, open Grocery: empty until you scroll past everything and press it.
- **The diff the row is supposed to show is erased by a form default.** `qtyInput.value = String(initial?.quantity ?? 1)` means the field is never blank, so `quantityBlank` is never true, so every add and every edit writes a `typicalOrder` row.
- **Neither list knows the other exists.** Strip the cream and this is Todoist with categories.

**Deterministic scan.** Three runs, all exit 0: `src/components` → 5, `index.html` → 0, `src` → 20. All 20 advisory, 0 blocking. By rule: `design-system-font-size` 11, `design-system-color` 6, `design-system-radius` 3. Findings 1–5 are five hits on one Instagram gradient declaration (`inspo-view.ts:15`) — a documented exception the detector honours for flat literals and breaks on for gradient stops. Finding 15 matches the `2px` inside `calc(var(--radius-sm) - 2px)`, a parser defect. The real signal is `components.css:99`: `.input-group label` at 12px uppercase with 0.5px tracking, below the project's own 13px floor and carrying the signage voice the world was rebuilt to remove, rendering inside the sheet both list views open.

## What's Working

1. **`mutate()`** (pantry-view.ts:74, grocery-view.ts:74). A failed write raises a toast with a Retry that re-runs the exact closure, plus an immediate `loadData()` so the optimistic UI snaps back to truth.
2. **The barcode failure taxonomy.** `BarcodeLookup` is a discriminated union of `found | unknown | offline | unavailable | cancelled`, with a 3-second aisle-appropriate timeout and a `handingOff` flag so a late lookup can't open a form after the user walked away.
3. **Measured token discipline.** Every Warm Paper text pair passes AA in both themes; `.kb-group-head` at 4.56:1 on the board is the thinnest margin and still passes. The out-of-stock state carries three redundant signals. Filling the tick with ink rather than green is right.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] Every item you add or edit becomes a permanent shopping staple
`item-form.ts:89` pre-fills the quantity field with `1`, so `quantityBlank` (line 153) is always `false`, so `saveUsual()` always writes to `typicalOrder`. Destroys the one signal PRODUCT.md mandates be legible on the row, and `regenerateGroceryList()` then emits an `auto` row for every one of them. Fix: an explicit 44px `aria-pressed` staple toggle, quantity revealed only when on and seeded from `row.usual`, never from `1`. → /impeccable shape

### [P0] The receipt scan is an irreversible bulk write with no progress, no review, and no undo
Disabled button with no disabled style → 2.5s toast → 5–30s of silence → writes to `pantryItems` and deletes from `groceryList` in one shot → "7 items moved", which seven never shown. `namesMatch()` uses bidirectional substring containment, so `MILK 2%` claims `oat milk`. Unmatched lines silently discarded. `createWorker('eng')` fetches worker, WASM core and language data from `cdn.jsdelivr.net`, and `dist/sw.js` precaches none of it — a third network dependency where PRODUCT.md allows two. Fix: real progress with Cancel; a review sheet showing the matching line per row; word-boundary matching; self-host and precache the assets. → /impeccable harden

### [P1] The product's whole mechanism is invisible and manually triggered
No route in (neither empty state names the typical order; the only door is Settings behind a 13px uppercase span with a 30px Add), no automatic derivation, and a trigger below the fold with the wrong label. Fix: derive on load and after every `typicalOrder` write, then delete the button; snooze a deliberately-deleted `auto` row until the item is next purchased; route into the mechanism from both empty states. → /impeccable shape

### [P1] Every primary action is at the top, and the toast lands on top of it
`.kb-toolbar` is `sticky; top: 0`. `.toast-container` is `top: calc(SAT + 8px); z-index: 300` with `pointer-events: auto`, so every confirmation covers the search field and the add button for 2.5–6 seconds, and Undo sits at the maximum distance from the thumb with a 5-second deadline. → /impeccable layout

### [P1] Nothing survives a tab switch
`router.render()` wipes `contentEl` and re-invokes the factory, which runs `renderSkeleton(); void loadData();`. Lost every time: search text, the out-only filter, scroll position. Gained: a flash of six grey bars over a 5ms read. → /impeccable polish

### [P1] Items in an unrecognised category are silently never rendered
Both views do `for (const cat of CATEGORIES)` and filter. `importData` does `store.put(row as any)` with no category validation. Rows vanish with no count, no notice, no error. → /impeccable harden

### [P2] No add path prevents duplicates
`addPantryItem`, `addManualGroceryItem` and `addTypicalOrderItem` all mint a UUID and `put()` unconditionally, while the `by-normalizedName` index sits created-and-never-queried and `getPantryNameSet()` is called by nothing. `updatePantryItem` recomputes `normalizedName` with no collision check. → /impeccable harden

### [P2] Two measured defects in Warm Paper's own rules
`.kb-row-main` — the Pantry edit target — computes to 26.1px tall (17px × 1.3 + 4px padding); `.kb-row` centres its children so it never stretches to 48px, and DESIGN.md's 44px Target Rule omits it. `.kb-add` has no visible focus ring: `--kb-ink` at `outline-offset: -3px` inside an `--kb-ink` fill measures **1.00:1**; the pressed out-filter is 2.42:1. Two load-bearing borders fail 1.4.11 — `.kb-check-box` at 1.60:1 light / 1.33:1 dark is the unchecked checkbox's entire affordance, and `.kb-btn` at 1.22:1 / 1.53:1 is a transparent button's only boundary. → /impeccable audit

## Persona Red Flags

**Casey — one hand, moving cart, interrupted.** Every action at the top; the app's only bottom control is on the Saved tab. Undo is the least reachable control and the only one on a timer. Toasts cover the toolbar and stack. `gotIt` has no undo. `outBtn.hidden = out === 0` reflows the toolbar mid-session. Pantry re-sorts on tick, so the row under the thumb changes identity right after a tap. No progress indicator.

**Riley — edge cases, sole user after six months.** Forty rows reading "usually buy 1". Duplicate join keys accumulating. `deletePantryItem` doesn't remove the `source: 'out'` row linked by `sourcePantryId`, and `regenerateGroceryList` preserves `out` rows, so deleting an out-of-stock item leaves an immortal list row. Nothing emits `grocery-count` at startup, so a cold launch shows no badge; `clearAllData` and `importData` don't emit either. `downloadJson` uses `a.download` with a blob URL — unreliable in an installed iOS PWA — and the "Data exported" toast fires unconditionally.

**Sam — VoiceOver, low vision, large text.** Exactly one heading element in the whole app (the modal title); no `h1` anywhere. Nine `<label>` elements, one associated; `for=`/`htmlFor` count is zero and no input has an `id`. `aria-label` on `.kb-row-main` overrides its children, hiding the "usually buy" line. Focus drops to `<body>` after every tick, delete and save. `aria-current` count: 0; the active tab is colour-only at 3.41:1. Three of six modal surfaces have no Escape, no focus trap, no focus restore, no dialog semantics. `-webkit-text-size-adjust: 100%` plus hard px means Dynamic Type does nothing.

## Minor Observations

- `saveLastChoices()` runs before `onSubmit`, so a failed write still changes the defaults. `parseFloat(qty) || 1` turns `0` into `1`.
- `emptyText()` tests `outOnly` before `query`, so a search with no hits inside the filter reports "You have everything."
- Grocery's `loadFailed` branch returns before `renderFooter()`, removing the only way to rebuild the list.
- Search matches `item.name` only, never `normalizedName`.
- Both `+` buttons are visually identical with the same `aria-label` and do materially different things.
- Grocery's `.kb-row-main` is a `div`, not a button.
- `.filter-pill` 29.5px, `.inspo-icon-btn` 28×28, `.modal-close` 28×28, `.btn-sm` 30px, `.toast-undo-btn` 19.5px — all under 44.
- Incumbent contrast, light: `.toast.success` **2.22:1**, `.toast.error` 3.55, `.tab-badge` 3.55, every `--color-text-secondary` body style 3.33–3.62. Dark inverts this cleanly, but the four meal-category badges are hardcoded light-mode values at 2.20–4.13:1 in both themes.
- The `.select` arrow has `fill='%2386868b'` frozen inside its data-URI.
- `.fab` is `position: fixed` inside the router's content element — clause 3 of the Floating Control Rule. It works only because `viewFadeIn` is opacity-only.
- Zero `safe-area-inset-left/right` anywhere, with `viewport-fit=cover` set.
- 21 dead CSS classes (~130 lines), 7 dead custom properties, dead service exports (`toggleGroceryItem`, `clearCheckedItems`, `getPantryItemsByCategory`, `getPantryNameSet`) and `clear`/`show`/`hide` in `dom.ts`. `.kb-btn--danger` is documented as shipped and rendered nowhere. No cascade-order defects — the old `.kb-btn--primary` bug is gone.
- Saved is broken by network reality: both oEmbed fetches are cross-origin with no timeout, and Meta's requires a token.

## Questions to Consider

1. If the list is derived, why is derivation a button? Is the missing primitive a dismissal rather than a button?
2. Is the shared sheet one form, or three forms wearing one component? The collision of "adding at a cupboard", "adding in an aisle" and "declaring a staple" is what produced the P0.
3. What is the record of a shopping trip? Ticking deletes, so a trip leaves no trace.
4. DESIGN.md accepts the two-system split for Saved and Settings. Should it accept it for the sheet and the toast — modal children of Warm Paper views that fire on every write?
5. Is Saved a real feature or the Recipes tab's ghost?
